# Khevra Grafts And Planar Strain

## Place In Aurevost

Cosmetic khevra grafts are widespread, not a rare reward gated behind a hunt.
They use fine khevra mixed into sands by erosion, processed into decorative
work. People wear them for fashion or status; individual access, price,
workmanship, law, and social response vary. A decorative piece can glow,
sound, or otherwise resonate with nearby khevric things, and elaborate work
may signal wealth or alignment. Such manifestations must come from an authored
graft and the observer's actual circumstances; appearance alone does not
reveal the wearer's exact contamination total.

Non-cosmetic grafts draw on veined creatures, including their cores. They are
rarer and carry a much higher individual contamination burden than ordinary
cosmetic work. The exact material requirement and cost belong to each graft;
do not assume that processing sand grants a functional augmentation or that
every core supplies an unlimited number of grafts. A cosmetic graft may have
zero cost; an appearance or resonance alone does not grant an unlisted
mechanical capability.

The deeper cause of accumulated strain is planar: khevra pulls a person
between planes. That is a setting truth, not automatically common knowledge.
Players learn only what their characters have observed or received through
authorized sources. Do not disclose hidden cosmology merely because a
character has a graft.

Players may select authored grafts at character creation using the active
game's starting-equipment and purchase rules, or seek them during play. A
graft is an optional choice, not a universal grant. The player reviews its
effect, price, individual contamination cost, known consequences, and
difficulty of removal before accepting it. If a necessary option, price,
starting limit, or fitting rule is missing, block that choice rather than
inventing a cost or silently granting the graft.

## Total And Tolerance

For each character, record separately:

- **Temporary exposure**, gained from authorized environmental, handling, or
  other effects and recoverable only through an authored recovery procedure.
- **Installed graft costs**, one nonnegative integer cost per installed graft.
  Some cosmetic grafts cost zero. A graft's cost persists while it is installed.
- **Total contamination**, the uncapped sum of temporary exposure and all
  installed graft costs.
- **Tolerance**, a guaranteed free baseline available to everyone plus an
  adjustment informed by Constitution and Wisdom. The exact formula, units,
  and treatment of ability changes still need to be authored.

At or below tolerance the character has **no contamination penalties**. Above
it, consequences grow nonlinearly across tiers. Calibrate these thresholds
against a catalog of example grafts so many modest grafts can be viable;
individual benefits and costs need not be equal. Neither total nor graft
count has a hard maximum. The highest defined tier is not a death sentence,
and additional points continue to be recorded even if no higher tier is
defined.

When a graft is fitted, add its listed installed cost once. Do not also add a
generic temporary exposure merely because it was fitted; an explicit fitting
procedure may impose its own temporary exposure. When an authorized recovery
reduces temporary exposure, the costs of installed grafts remain. If an
authorized removal succeeds, remove only that graft's cost and effects, then
recompute the total; it does not automatically clear temporary exposure or
other grafts. Missing cost, capacity, recovery, or removal rules do not
silently become zero or success.

## Consequences And Perception

As strain increases, a person's perception may become less reliable and
mechanical complications may become more significant. The precise trigger,
scope, frequency, duration, counters, and tier-specific penalties need
authored rules. They should make higher burden matter without forcing a PC's
choices, permanently handing the PC to the DM, or making contamination itself
automatically lethal. Ordinary physical dangers still follow their own rules;
this is not immunity to injury from unrelated causes.

The condition definition's table visibility does not make an actor's condition
instance, numerical total, or any perception automatically table-visible.
Choose and record their authorized audiences separately under the world
visibility rules. Private perceptions stay with their intended audience. An
apparent sound, figure, or location is a perception or claim with a source and
truth status, not an automatic change to shared reality or proof of a secret.
The player chooses the character's response. Safety boundaries apply to the
way disorientation is presented.

Grafts bind magically to the body, so removal is **very challenging**, not
the inverse of taking off equipment. An actual procedure must establish the
fitter, consent, prerequisites, time, cost, risks, and the consequences for
the graft and character. Failure, permanent loss, or death are not automatic
defaults; resolve only authored outcomes. Neither the system nor an NPC
performs a fitting or removal on a PC without the applicable consent and
authority.

## Worked Accounting Fixture

This is a numerical fixture for testing accounting, **not** a proposed
capacity formula, price, threshold, or approved catalog:

1. A character has a hypothetical tolerance of 3, no temporary exposure, a
   cosmetic graft costing 0, and functional grafts costing 1 and 2. Total is
   `0 + 1 + 2 = 3`: no contamination penalty.
2. One independently authorized exposure raises temporary exposure to 1 and
   total to 4. The character is above the fixture's tolerance, but the
   resulting tier and effect are unknown until the real thresholds exist.
3. If an independently authorized, successful removal of the cost-1 graft is
   stipulated, installed costs total 2 and temporary exposure remains 1.
   Total is 3 again. This example does not define or guarantee removal.

Each fitting, exposure, recovery, and removal requires accepted rules-owned
state changes, source and before/after values, and atomic treatment of linked
effects. Concrete state/event contracts and replay are not yet authored.

## Open Authoring Work

- The guaranteed baseline and Constitution/Wisdom tolerance formula, including
  ability changes and non-5e compatibility.
- Nonlinear tiers and specific, nonlethal complications, calibrated against
  varied cosmetic, practical, and powerful graft examples. Test representative
  portfolios for ordinary and unusually resilient characters, including the
  effect of temporary exposure when graft costs already fill tolerance.
- A catalog distinguishing sand-derived cosmetic and veined-derived functional
  work, with effects, resonance, costs, price, starting eligibility, fitting
  and difficult removal; rules for overlapping graft effects. Decide deliberately
  whether different cosmetic manifestations should have different costs.
- Temporary exposure recovery; whether individual graft procedures add
  temporary exposure; and how state changes, sources, and private perceptions
  replay without leaking hidden truth.

The [budget calibration scenario](simulations/scenarios/graft_budget_calibration.yaml)
compares hypothetical grafts, a tolerance formula, and nonlinear thresholds.
Its numbers are test inputs, not an accepted catalog or playable rules.
